Make it actionable

What a report needs for us to act on it

Include this

  • The release you are on
  • Which MCP row was enabled, and whether it appeared in the panel at all
  • The exact wording of any error, copied rather than paraphrased
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

Leave this out

  • Real controller credentials or a plant network path
  • A customer’s proprietary program file, unless you have permission to share it
  • A full screenshot of a plant HMI showing production data

A trimmed export that still reproduces the problem is the most useful file you can send.
